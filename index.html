I’m Pritesh Mayekar, an art director and visualiser working across brand identity, packaging, campaigns and immersive visual worlds.

My work connects a strong central idea to every expression around it—from a pack on a shelf to a world on a screen. I combine design craft with AI-assisted visual exploration to give ideas form, scale and character.
